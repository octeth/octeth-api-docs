# Sender Domain Health

<Badge type="tip" text="New in v6.0.1" />

The Sender Domain Health report moved to the staff area of the new interface in v6.0.2. The classic admin **Reports > Sender Domain Health** menu item and the old `/app/admin/senderdomainhealth/` addresses open it there, with their filters applied.

See [The Staff Sender Domain Health Report](/v6.0.4/new-user-interface/staff-sender-domain-health).
